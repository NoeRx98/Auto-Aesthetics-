Visitors can't see what paint correction or paintless dent repair actually does. Each piece below shows one service working, in a way a photo can't. Every one is interactive: press Play, or drag the slider to run it yourself. They're built with plain canvas and CSS. No animation library, nothing to download.

The motion rules allow one signature animation per landing page. That slot now goes to the request journey on the page itself; the three explainers below sit inside the service cards and play only when tapped.

01Inside the clear coat

Shows: why correction lasts and a glaze doesn't
Cross-section · not to scale

What the audience gets

Scratches are grooves in the clear coat. Correction levels the clear coat down to the bottom of each groove, so there's nothing left to fill and nothing to wash off. Cheap shops fill the grooves with glaze, and the swirls come back after a few washes.

Where it goes

Inside the paint correction service card, behind "How it works". It plays when tapped.

Limits

Check the wording with the shop. Say only what they actually do, for example whether they measure paint depth before cutting.

02Reading the dent

Shows: paintless dent repair · factory paint stays
Reflection board on the panelFactory paint · untouched

What the audience gets

PDR technicians read a dent by how straight lines bend in its reflection. Watch the lines bend around the dent, then straighten as small pushes from behind work it out, with no filler and no paint.

Where it goes

The dent repair card, and the landing page for a dent or hail ad. On that page it can be the signature moment.

Motion cost

Tier 1–2 · canvas · about 2 KB.

Limits

It shows a clean, shallow dent. Creased or stretched metal may need more than PDR, so keep "can be same day" as "can be".
