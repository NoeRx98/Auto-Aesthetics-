Six ways to make the photos work harder.

Every frame here is a real photo from your shop. Nothing is generated or faked. Each module uses a different design and motion idea, and under it is what it does for the business, where it would go and what it costs in speed. Scroll slowly; several of these react to scrolling, the pointer or touch.

02Light pass

Pointer-driven light · GT 53 rear
Look at the reflections.Move across the car · Mercedes-AMG GT 53

What it does

A shop light glides across the paint and follows the pointer. It makes visitors look at the reflections, which is how owners judge a finish.

Where

The GT 53 showcase card on hover, or a full-width break between sections.

Motion

Tier 1 · hover / touch · CSS

It moves only when the visitor moves. Nothing loops.

Watch out

It's an effect on a photo, not a before/after. Don't caption it as a result.

03Look closer

Magnifier · carbon hood detail
Drag or hover
Carbon fiber restoration

For owners who notice everything.

The magnifier invites the visitor to inspect the weave the way they'd inspect their own car. It's a confident move: you only hand someone a magnifying glass when the work holds up.

What it does

It turns your best detail shot into an inspection. Confidence reads as quality.

Where

The carbon service card, or a carbon-only ad landing page.

Motion

Tier 1 · visitor-driven

It follows the pointer or a finger. The lens rests on the weave so the first frame is complete.

Watch out

It needs a sharp, high-resolution original. Upload the full-size photo for the real build.

04Made by hand

Staggered reveal · technician photos

Real hands. Real bay.

The work is done by the people in these photos, in this workshop, on your car.

What it does

It puts people on the page. Owners hand their car to a person, not a logo, and this answers "who touches my car?".

Where

Just before the quote form, where trust has to peak.

Motion

Tier 1 · CSS

The three frames settle into place once as they enter the screen, staggered in height like a contact sheet.

Watch out

Add a name under one photo. "Masked by Marco" beats "Masked by hand".

05Matte study

Scrollytelling · G-Class photos
Mercedes-Benz G-Class
01

Matte paint can't be polished like gloss.

On a matte finish, polishing creates shiny spots. There's no buffing out a mistake, so the work has to be right the first time.

02

Every panel has to match in sheen as well as color.

Panels, bumpers and trim are checked by hand, at the angles an owner actually looks from.

03

Then it goes back outside, in real light.

The final check happens in daylight, where the finish has to look right every day.

What it does

It teaches one hard-won fact (matte paint can't be polished) and ties it to your work. Expertise someone learns from you is the kind they trust.

Where

A feature section on the full site, or the landing page for a matte paint ad.

Motion

Tier 2 · signature if used

The photo stays pinned while three short beats scroll past and swap it.

Watch out

Confirm what was done on the G-Class before this ships. Beats 02 and 03 describe your process; change anything that isn't how you work.

The ceiling on all of this is the photos. These designs make good photos look great, but they can't fix a cluttered background. The next shoot needs a clean backdrop, the finished bumper on the car, and one portrait with a name.
