Your questions, answered

What types of vehicles do you work on?

We specialize in luxury, exotic and high-performance vehicles: Mercedes-AMG, Porsche, Ferrari, Lamborghini, Rolls-Royce and the like. That said, our standard applies to any vehicle our clients cherish.

What does carbon fiber restoration involve?

Sun-faded, yellowed, hazed or lightly scratched clear coat over your carbon weave is stripped, corrected and re-cleared to a deep, glass-clear gloss, bringing the original weave back to life.

How long does the process take?

It depends entirely on scope. A paintless dent repair can be same-day; carbon fiber restoration is typically 2 to 3 days; a show-quality respray is a multi-week process. Your proposal includes an accurate timeline.

Do you work with insurance claims?

Yes. We work with insurance providers on covered repairs and act as your advocate so the work is completed to our standard, not the insurer's minimum.

What happens after I submit the form?

One of our representatives reviews your vehicle details and reaches out on the channel and time window you chose, usually within a few business hours, with a transparent, no-obligation quote.
